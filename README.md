# Tide & Trove — Made for the Dar Al Fikr AI Competition

Tide & Trove is a museum management sim that aims to teach people about fossils. It was vibe-coded entirely with Claude Code, built in Godot 4.7.2 with GDScript, and made for the Dar Al Fikr AI Competition 2026.

## Q&A

**Q: What is this game about?**
**A:** Tide & Trove is a museum management game that teaches you about history and fossils. You dig up fossils around Ammonite Bay and display them in your museum to attract visitors. Earn money for upgrades and better equipment, or just collect fossils and read about them. The choice is yours!

**Q: What AI do you use and how much does it cost?**
**A:** We use Claude Code for all the coding and modeling. You need a Claude subscription; ours is the cheapest plan, at $20 (before tax).

## How to play

Download the latest build from the [Releases page](https://github.com/BlobberLikesDucks/tide-and-trove/releases). It's available for Windows and macOS.

**Windows:**
1. Download `TideAndTrove-…-windows-x64.zip` and extract it.
2. Double-click `TideAndTrove.exe`.
3. If Windows warns the app is unsafe, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**macOS:**
1. Download `TideAndTrove-…-macos-universal.zip` and double-click it to extract it.
2. Drag **Tide & Trove.app** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open **Terminal** (Applications → Utilities → Terminal) and paste:
   ```
   xattr -cr "/Applications/Tide & Trove.app"
   ```
4. Open the game. If macOS still asks, right-click it, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
