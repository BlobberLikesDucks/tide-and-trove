# Tide & Trove — The Secret of Ammonite Bay

A first-person incremental museum-management sim built from `docs/GDD_Tide_and_Trove.md`.
It uses **Godot 4.7 (GDScript, Jolt Physics)** for the game and **Blender 5.2** for every 3D
mesh, including the ground, sea, roads, roofs and buildings. Every UI element is **Canva**
art: logo, ribbon, panels, cards, pills, buttons, bars, sliders, checkboxes, stars, crosshair,
walkie-talkie frame and icons. Music and SFX are synthesized by `tools/gen_audio.py`.

## Scenes, components and look

- **Logo:** the taskbar/window icon and the boot splash use the Canva-made Tide & Trove logo
  (`game/assets/branding/`, multi-size `icon.ico` for Windows exports). The Godot startup
  logo is replaced.
- **Cel shading:** `assets/shaders/cel.gdshader` (banded light, cool shadow tint, toon
  highlight, rim) plus `cel_outline.gdshader` (inverted-hull ink outline).
  - `Art.toon` converts every painted Blender material to this pair.
  - Floors stay matte and ground-sized meshes get no outline.
- **Editor-built scenes:** the static world is laid out in the editor rather than generated by
  scripts.
  - `scenes/world.tscn`: environment, sun, ground, town, shop, lab, museum frontage, beach
    dressing and world bounds (about 340 nodes).
  - `scenes/actors/player.tscn`: capsule, head, camera, view-model arms and look ray.
  - `scenes/main_menu.tscn`: the menu diorama, plus the menu UI (logo, button panel, tagline).
  - `scenes/ui/game_ui.tscn`: the HUD.
    - Status and rating cards, objective and prompt pills, crosshair, toasts.
    - Walkie-talkie, hotbar, detector bar and hints.
    - The panel layer and the dialog box.
  - `scenes/ui/modal.tscn`: the shared panel frame (dim, Canva panel, ribbon title, close
    button, body). Every panel instances it and fills its body with live data.
  - The UI scenes use `data/ui/ui_theme.res`, the Canva theme with type variations such as
    `CardPanel`, `PillPanel`, `WalkiePanel`, the colour bars, the colour buttons and
    `PillLabel`. They look the same in the editor as in game.
  - To change the theme, edit `UIKit.build_theme()` and re-run `tools/export_ui_theme.tscn`.
  - Scripts only spawn runtime things: guests, staff, dig nodes, placed exhibits, and the
    museum walls (which follow the purchased floor size).
- **Components** (`scripts/components/`), configured in the Inspector:
  - `InteractSpot`: prompt plus the method to call on Interact.
  - `AutoCollider`: fits a collider when the model has no Blender proxy.
  - `WaterSurface`: the animated sea shader.
- **Tuning:** `data/game_config.tres` (`GameConfig`) holds movement, inventory sizes, debt
  limits, queue patience, vendor speed, bin capacity, plaque penalties and outline settings.
  Edit it in the Inspector, no code changes.

## Run it

1. Open `game/project.godot` in Godot 4.7 and press **F5**.
2. Or from a terminal:
   ```
   Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path game
   ```

## The loop

1. **Prep phase (time paused).** Your **hotbar** (3 slots, +1 per star from the Upgrade Tree)
   holds what's in your hand: select the shovel and press **LMB** on a sand patch, or the pickaxe on
   rock. While you hold the detector, it pings over buried finds. **Q** drops the held item and **X**
   trashes it, but the shovel, pickaxe and detector are vital and can't be trashed. The **gear rack**
   in the Prep Lab re-issues any tool you dropped or lost.
   - Sell gems and ores at Pearl's **Equipment Shop**.
   - Open the **storage crates** to move single items between hotbar, backpack and storage, and to
     see your cleaned fossils.
   - Clean rock-encased fossils at the **Prep Lab** workbench. Mount them in display cases and pick
     each **plaque**'s Era, Diet and Habitat. There is no auto option: a wrong plaque makes visitors
     complain, and fossil enthusiasts post savage one-star reviews that count double. A cheap
     **Fact-Check Kiosk** (buildable on day one) lists every wrong or blank plaque.
   - Arrange the floor in **Build Mode**. Items you can't afford are greyed out.
   - **Amber** is a fossil now (an Eocene termite in resin), not a gem.
2. **Flip the sign by the door → Open phase.** Time runs and visitors arrive. Seven archetypes visit:
   tourists, rowdy kids, enthusiasts, celebrities, critics, shoplifters and corporate spies. The
   **walkie-talkie** calls out incidents while you dig.
   - Hungry guests **queue at snack stands** and wait for you. Look at the stand and press E three
     times: grab a cup, bag or bun; fill it from the jug, machine or grill; serve the guest at the front.
     Guests who wait too long (about 45 s) give up and complain in their review.
     A hired **Snack Vendor** serves the queues for you.
   - Guests drop trash. Pick it up (E), then throw it in a bin (E while looking at the bin).
3. **Sunset.** **Daily reviews** move the Quality meter. At 100% the museum gains half a star.
   The **ledger** then pays wages, loans and upkeep. Falling below −$1,000 means bankruptcy.
4. **Spend Upgrade Points in the Upgrade Tree (T).** You earn them from half-stars, new species,
   5-star review days, critic commendations and field-trip trivia.
   - The tree is a real node graph split into **star tiers (0-5)**. A tier unlocks only at that many
     museum stars.
   - Lines connect parents to children: buy a node's parents first, and some nodes unlock several
     branches.
   - Click a node to see its name, description and price, with Unlock at the bottom. Bigger nodes
     cost cash as well as UP.

**Story.** Act 1: rags to riches. Act 2: the first bone of the apex reptile turns up in the Sea Caves.
Act 3: Grimsby Extraction Co. sends spies. **You win** at 5★ with the complete *Pelagotyrannus* skeleton.

## Staff: slots, morale and quitting

- You start with **0 staff slots**. Every star tier from 2 stars up has a **Staff Slot +1** node.
- Each job is its own licence in the tree: **Janitor** and **Snack Vendor** (1 star),
  **Security Guard** (2 stars) and **Paleontology Intern** (3 stars).
  - The intern corrects every plaque each morning.
  - The vendor walks to whichever snack stand has a queue and serves one guest every 3.5 s.
- Every employee has **morale** (shown in Management → Staff with a heart and a bar).
- **Morale goes down** when you shove them (−30, or −20 if they were rogue), reprimand them (−8),
  bounce a paycheck (−15 for everyone) or have a terrible review day (−6). It also slips a little
  every working hour.
- **Morale goes up** with Praise (+10, once a day), a Raise (+$10/day wage, +22 morale, up to 3),
  great review days (+5) and a night's rest (+4).
- Low morale makes staff go rogue more often. At 0 they **quit on the spot**: they radio in a
  resignation line and walk out of town. Very unhappy staff may also hand in their notice at the
  ledger, which lists **Resignations**.

## Art pipeline (Blender skills)

Every 3D asset (102 GLBs) is built by `blender/build.py` using the **blender-skills**
production pipeline (blender-director workflow, genre-strategy-sim + stylized-style art
direction). The kit lives in `blender/tt/`, and the assets live in `blender/ttassets/`:

| Family | Module | Skills applied |
|---|---|---|
| Museum kit, town, lab, grounds, cliffs, cave, sea | `env.py` | environment-artist, procedural-modeling, geometry-nodes (scatter), set-dressing |
| Palms, ferns, grass, rocks, driftwood, dig nodes | `nature.py` | vegetation-artist, sculpting (normal bakes) |
| Furniture, exhibits, attractions, food carts, decor, held tools | `props.py` | prop-artist, collision-proxy |
| Gems, fossils, living fossils, mega-skeletons | `specimens.py` | sculpting, creature proportions (true tailbends, eye rings) |
| Characters (body, head, hair, outfits, rig, 14 clips) + first-person arms | `characters.py` | character-artist, character-archetypes, retopology, rigging, hair-groom, animation |
| FX particle meshes + set-dressing clusters | `fxdress.py` | vfx-fx, set-dressing, scene-assembly |

Each asset goes through these steps:

1. **Model.**
2. **UV unwrap.** Shared atlas; scattered props get their own trim-sheet tiles.
3. **Optional sculpt pass.** A displaced high-poly copy is baked into a normal map.
4. **Hand-painted texture bake** (Cycles):
   - a painted top-light gradient;
   - cool cavity shadows;
   - warm edge highlights;
   - brush mottling;
   - material strokes (wood grain, strata bands, speckle, weave).
5. **Material merge.** All painted materials collapse into one `MAT_<Asset>` (one draw call). The tint materials keep their names so Godot can recolour them.
6. **Collision proxies.** Exported as `COL_*-convcolonly` meshes; Godot imports them as StaticBody3D, replacing the hand-written colliders.
7. **Validation.** Triangle budget per tier, UVs, loose verts, material count and scale, written to `blender/qa/report_*.json`.
8. **GLB export.**
9. **QA contact sheet** in `blender/qa/*.png`.

## Characters

`character.glb` is a stylized, roughly 5.5-head-tall base body built as follows:

- **Body:** a Skin-modifier blockout gives an all-quad mesh with joint rings. A sculpt shaping pass follows, then automatic weights limited to 4 influences.
- **Head:** big eyes, brows, nose, ears and blush.
- **Hair:** hair-card clumps (short, long, bun, and an under-hat variant).
- **Outfit pieces**, toggled per archetype:
  - Hats: safari, cap, fedora, top hat.
  - Glasses and sunglasses, beard.
  - Backpack, tie, photo camera, staff lanyard, clipboard.
  - Hi-vis vest and coat. These are clothing shells duplicated from the body so they inherit its weights.
- **Rig:** the skeleton uses Mixamo-style names (Hips, Spine, Chest, Neck, Head, Left/Right Shoulder/Arm/ForeArm/Hand/UpLeg/Leg/Foot).
- **Animations:** 14 clips keyed in Blender (the wave raises the forearm from a shoulder-height elbow and rocks the hand) (`blender/tt/anims.py`):
  - Loops: `AN_Idle`, `AN_Walk`, `AN_Run`, `AN_Dig`, `AN_Sweep`, `AN_Tantrum`, `AN_Sit`, `AN_Sleep`, `AN_Wave`, `AN_Cheer`, `AN_Climb`, `AN_Inspect`.
  - One-shots: `AN_Kick`, `AN_Getup`.

`CharVisual.clip()` maps game states to these clips. Any MeshyAI or Mixamo rig with the same bone and clip names can be swapped in through `CharVisual.MODEL_ID`.

**First-person arms** (`fp_arms.glb`) are two sleeved forearms with modelled fists. A `GripR` /
`GripL` point sits at the centre of each fist, so held tools, finds, trash and cups sit in the hand.
Ten clips are keyed in Blender and merged per NLA track on export:

- `AN_FP_Idle`, `AN_FP_HoldTool`, `AN_FP_HoldPick`, `AN_FP_HoldItem`
- `AN_FP_Dig`, `AN_FP_Mine`, `AN_FP_Detector`
- `AN_FP_Shove`, `AN_FP_Throw`, `AN_FP_Grab`

Both arms are always on screen, so the left hand never pops in. It stays on the shaft for
two-handed tools and rests low otherwise.

Signs carry real **3D lettering** made in Blender with `tt.geo.text3d`: BUS, TIDE & TROVE, EQUIPMENT &
SUPPLIES, PREP LAB, OPEN/CLOSED, MUSEUM and PERMIT REQUIRED. There are no floating text labels.

## Ragdolls

When a character is knocked over:

1. Eleven skeleton bones each become their own `RigidBody3D`: hips, spine, head, upper and lower arms, thighs and shins.
2. Joints link them: cone-twist joints at the hips, shoulders, neck and spine, and limited hinge joints at the knees and elbows.
3. The skinned skeleton copies the bodies every frame, and a puff of Blender dust FX plays.
4. Once the hips settle, the character stands where it landed and plays the `AN_Getup` clip.

## Audio

`tools/gen_audio.py` is a numpy synthesizer rendering 44.1 kHz stereo:

- **Loops:** three seamless tracks (circular reverb). They share one "Tide & Trove" theme.
  - **Prep:** ukulele, marimba and surf.
  - **Open:** steel pan, calypso bass and kit.
  - **Closing:** electric piano, music box and brushes.
- **Instruments:** Karplus-Strong plucks, FM steel pan, electric piano, marimba and bells, detuned pads, and a synthesized drum kit.
- **SFX:** 22 layered effects that share the same reverb space.

## Automated test

```
Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --path game -- --autotest
```

The test plays a full day. It covers digging, selling, cleaning, skills, building, expansion,
staff slots (0 at start, then bought), save/load, guests, reviews, ledger and the next day. It also
sweeps every panel, build placement, the first-person shove, the 11-body ragdoll and getting back
up, praise and raises, an employee quitting, the story acts, the win condition and bankruptcy.
The test also covers:

- the hotbar: holding, dropping, picking up, trashing (vital tools refused) and the gear rack;
- moving items between hotbar, backpack and storage;
- tree rules: parents first, star tiers, cash prices, Tool Belt +1;
- job licences, and a Snack Vendor serving a queue on their own;
- blank and wrong plaques and the Fact-Check Kiosk;
- serving a queued guest at a stand.

It uses (and overwrites) save slot 10. Add `--shots --out=DIR` (without `--headless`) to save
screenshots.

## Design decisions (from the GDD knowledge graph)

- The GDD's "4-stage" day cycle lists Closing twice. It is implemented as **Prep → Open → Reviews → Ledger**.
- "Skill Tree" and "Upgrade Tree" are one system. "Research Assistant" and "Paleontology Intern" are one staff role.
- Characters share one rigged base body; archetypes are outfit pieces + material tints, so a MeshyAI rig can still replace it.
- The apex reptile *Pelagotyrannus covensis* is fictional and labelled as such in-game. All other species use real-world facts.
